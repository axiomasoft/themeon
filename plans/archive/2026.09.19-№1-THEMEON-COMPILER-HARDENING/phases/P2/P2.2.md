### P2.2 — Targeted mutation gates for critical logic

**Intent:** Measure test sensitivity in named critical modules and gate only stable, actionable surviving mutants.

**Why:** Coverage alone cannot show whether resolver/security/diagnostic assertions detect incorrect behavior.

**Scope Included:** Reuse/tool check; mutation config for graph/resolver/tenant/diagnostic decisions; baseline MSI; survivor triage and lane budget.

**Scope Excluded:** Whole-repository mutation on every PR; arbitrary score target; changing semantics to kill mutants.

**Inputs:** `phases/P0/P0.3.state.md`; `phases/P0/P0.6.state.md`; `phases/P1/P1.3.state.md`; `phases/P1/P1.4.state.md`; `phases/P2/P2.1.md`; `phases/P2/P2.1.state.md`

**Files:** Mutation config/scripts; focused tests; `.github/workflows/**`; `docs/testing/mutation.md`.

**Required Reads:** `plan.md`; `phases/P2/P2.md`; `phases/P2/P2.1.md`; `phases/P2/P2.1.state.md`; `phases/P0/P0.md`; `packages/core/src/resolve.ts`; `packages/core/src/patch.ts`; `packages/core/src/errors.ts`; `vitest.config.ts`; `.github/workflows/ci.yml`

**Implementation Rules:** Equivalent/time-out mutants are classified with evidence; threshold follows measured baseline; critical survivors block or receive explicit issue/decision.

**Code Guidance:** Mutate a narrow allowlist first; run periodic/release lane unless measured PR cost is acceptable; preserve raw report artifact.

**Validation:** Baseline and threshold reproducible; one known mutation fails the gate; focused tests/root checks green; lane timeout documented.

**Deliverables:** Mutation lane, baseline/policy, survivor ledger and strengthened critical tests.

# D4 — Ecosystem remains consumer-gated

**Actor:** plan-designer/gpt-5.6-sol/high
**Evidence:** RAG:— `ROADMAP.md`; `audits/2026-09-19-audit.md` sections “Новые пакеты” and “P4 — Ecosystem”

## Решение

This plan does not authorize new framework adapters, Composer/Blade integration, preset registry,
React/Svelte packages or a manufactured “real pilot”. Existing ROADMAP triggers remain binding.

## Почему

The repository already records a deliberate anti-overengineering policy. An audit recommendation
does not prove a consumer exists and cannot silently replace the owner-defined trigger.

## Consequences

P3 may improve generic compiled artifacts, Vite/Laravel delivery and conformance fixtures, but any
triggered ecosystem product receives a separate plan and evidence after the trigger fires.
